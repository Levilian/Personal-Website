On evaluation

I work on AI evaluation, specifically knowledge work.

A few domains: life sciences, medical devices, energy / oil and gas, legal / finance.

I run Raycaster, which develops new benchmarks and environments for life science and med device work. I also support evalscience.org, a nonprofit focused on the science of agent evaluation.

Friends know me as an existential pragmatic with a daoist metaphysical temperament and a tragic-humanist sense of life.
